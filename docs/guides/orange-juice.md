# Orange Juice

Freshly squeezed orange juice takes only a few minutes and tastes far better than anything from a carton. This guide covers choosing the right oranges, juicing methods, and a few ways to serve it.

{% hint style="info" %}
Juice oranges at room temperature — cold fruit yields noticeably less juice than fruit left out for 30 minutes.
{% endhint %}

## What you need

- Oranges (navel or Valencia work well)
- Citrus juicer (manual or electric)
- Strainer (optional, if you prefer pulp-free juice)
- Glass or jug

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
#### Choose your oranges

Pick oranges that feel heavy for their size — weight indicates juice content. Thin-skinned varieties like **Valencia** are especially juicy; **navel oranges** are sweeter and easier to find year-round.

Plan on **2–3 medium oranges per 200 ml glass**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Roll and cut

Place each orange on a hard surface and press down firmly while rolling it back and forth for a few seconds. This breaks down the internal membranes and releases more juice.

Cut each orange in half across the middle, not through the stem ends.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Juice

Press each half onto the juicer and twist firmly. Work from the outside edge inward to extract as much juice as possible without pressing too hard, which can release bitter compounds from the pith.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Avoid grinding the rind against the juicer. Pith and rind add bitterness that builds up across multiple oranges.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Strain (optional)

Pour through a fine strainer if you prefer less pulp. For a middle ground, use a coarse strainer that keeps some pulp but removes seeds and larger fibres.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
#### Serve

Pour over ice or serve immediately at room temperature. Freshly squeezed juice oxidises quickly — drink within **20–30 minutes** for peak flavour and colour.

Add a pinch of salt to amplify sweetness without adding sugar.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

## Variations

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="With pulp" %}
Skip the strainer entirely. The pulp adds texture and retains more fibre. Stir before drinking as pulp settles to the bottom.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Blended" %}
Quarter the oranges, remove seeds, and blend with a small amount of water. Strain through a fine mesh for a smoother result with slightly more body than juiced-only.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="With mint" %}
Add 3–4 fresh mint leaves to the glass before pouring the juice. Lightly press the leaves against the side of the glass to release the oils without shredding them.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

<details>
<summary>Storing fresh juice</summary>

Keep in an airtight container in the fridge and use within **24 hours**. Juice loses vitamin C and flavour quickly once squeezed.

Avoid storing in a glass that is only partially full — the extra air speeds up oxidisation.
</details>
